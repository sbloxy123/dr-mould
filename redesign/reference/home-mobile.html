About us

Proper treatment, not just a clean-up

Dr Mould started in 2024 to do one job properly: mould removal and cleaning. Whether it’s a patch on a bathroom ceiling or a whole wall, we work to a high standard, and our aim on every job is to leave the area looking like new.

We know how stubborn mould can be once it’s had time to grow beneath the surface. We use specialist equipment, full PPE and proven techniques, and always explain what’s causing the problem.

What we do

From first spot to long-term fix

Mould comes back if the cause isn’t dealt with. Every job covers removal, treatment and practical advice.

Mould removal

Safe, thorough removal of black mould and other types from walls, ceilings, wardrobes and cupboards.

Finding hidden mould

Mould can hide behind walls, under floors and in roof spaces. We inspect and track down where the damp is coming from.

Treatment & prevention

We treat affected areas to stop regrowth and advise on ventilation, humidity and insulation so it stays away.

Check areas prone to moisture, such as bathrooms, kitchens and around leaks. Black mould usually shows as black or dark green patches and can feel slimy.
